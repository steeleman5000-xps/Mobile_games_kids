🃏 How to Play Level 8

Goal: Complete all 8 phases before the computer!

Each turn:

  1. Tap the Draw pile or the Discard to take a card.
  2. (Optional) Tap cards in your hand to select them, then press Lay Phase when they match the goal.
  3. (Optional) If you've laid your phase, tap cards then Play on Phase to extend sets/runs.
  4. Tap ONE card, then press Discard Selected to end your turn.

Sets = same number (any color). Runs = consecutive numbers (e.g., 4‑5‑6‑7).

Wilds 🌈 can stand in for any card.

The first player to finish a round and lay Phase 8 wins the game! 🏆
